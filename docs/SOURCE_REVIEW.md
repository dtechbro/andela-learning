Schema validation verifies IDs, references, source fields, criteria, and valid unique options. Automated tests verify two alternative questions per concept.

## Limits and future review

The question bank is deliberately small. It does not cover all introductory biology or any specific university exam. Written responses are self-assessed, not automatically graded. Questions have not been trialled with students or approved by a lecturer. Obtain subject-expert feedback before expanding or making stronger educational claims. Recheck licence and attribution when changing sources or reuse context.
